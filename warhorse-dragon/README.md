# The Warhorse vs. the Opal Dragon

A companion to [The Glory Paladin vs. the Opal Dragon](../paladin-dragon/). The paladin's black warhorse (white mane, grey dapples, still wearing his saddle and bridle) stands up on his hind legs and fights the opalescent dragon in his pasture.

Open `index.html` in a browser. It loads three.js r128 from cdnjs, so it needs an internet connection.

The fight runs as a 28-second loop in seven beats:

1. **Rude Awakening** – the dragon lands in his pasture. He looks up from the grass and stands up.
2. **Squaring Off** – he cracks his neck, raises his hooves and snorts.
3. **Prismatic Breath** – he leans back and lets the breath pass over him.
4. **One-Two** – two jabs and a hoof uppercut to the jaw.
5. **Tail Whip** – he jumps over the dragon's tail.
6. **Double Buck** – he turns around, plants his front hooves and kicks with both hind legs.
7. **Back to Grazing** – the dragon flies off. He flexes, drops to all fours and goes back to eating grass.

Controls: drag to orbit the camera, scroll or pinch to zoom, space to pause. The buttons along the bottom jump to each beat, and there is a slow-motion toggle.

A rendered video of one full loop is in [`../videos/warhorse-vs-opal-dragon.mp4`](../videos/warhorse-vs-opal-dragon.mp4).
