# The Eternal Loaf

A medieval idle game in the spirit of *Universal Paperclips*. Open `index.html` in any browser. Progress saves automatically in the browser.

You start with one button, **Bake a Loaf**, and a stall in the market square. Sell bread, hire apprentices, win the favor of the Bakers' Guild, and let your scribes' wisdom unlock new edicts. In time the bakery becomes a realm, the realm becomes a fleet, and the fleet sails off the edge of the map.

**Phase I: The Stall.** Set your price against public demand, buy flour from a miller whose price drifts, hire apprentices and master bakers, pay the town crier. Guild favor, earned at Fibonacci milestones, buys scribes (wisdom speed) or scrolls (wisdom capacity). Wisdom and inspiration pay for projects. Later come the Counting House (merchant ventures) and the Alchemist's Crucibles (click while they glow).

**Phase II: The Realm.** Silver is gone. Spend loaves on reapers, millers, and bakehouses to turn the kingdom's fields into bread, and build chapels to keep the enthralled peasants working.

**Phase III: The Great Voyage.** Launch arks, assign zeal to their priorities, found colonies, and deal with the heretic fleets.

A full playthrough takes roughly three hours of active play. The game also progresses while the tab is closed, for up to four hours.
