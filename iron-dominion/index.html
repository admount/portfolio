Iron Dominion

Raise a village from three peasants and a hall. Gather food, wood, gold and stone, advance through four ages, and break every rival banner on the map.

How to play

Select with left click or drag a box. Shift adds to the selection. Double-click selects every unit of that kind on screen.

Command with right click: move, attack, gather a tree, mine, forage berries, farm, or build and repair your own structures.

Build by selecting villagers, then pick Economy or Military buildings. Hold Shift to place several. Drag to lay walls.

Drop-offs. Villagers carry 10 at a time back to a Town Center, Lumber Camp, Mining Camp or Mill. Build camps near forests and mines.

Farms need a Mill. Houses add 5 population each, up to 200.

Advance at the Town Center. Each age unlocks buildings, units and upgrades.

Counters. Spearmen beat cavalry, skirmishers beat archers, archers beat infantry, knights beat archers, rams and trebuchets break walls and towers.

Keys. Command grid uses Q W E R T / A S D F G / Z X C V B. Ctrl+1–9 saves a group. H Town Center, . idle villager, Del delete, P pause, arrows or screen edge scroll, wheel zooms.

Victory. Destroy every enemy unit and building, or complete a Wonder and hold it for 5 minutes.

Touch. Drag with one finger to pan, tap to select, then tap the ground or a target to command.
