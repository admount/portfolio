# The Glory Paladin vs. the Opal Dragon

A 3D animation, built with [three.js](https://threejs.org/), of an orc paladin (Oath of Glory) under a *Fly* spell fighting an opalescent dragon with a shadow longsword.

Open `index.html` in a browser. It loads three.js r128 from cdnjs, so it needs an internet connection.

The fight runs as a 28-second loop in seven beats:

1. **Flight** – the *Fly* spell lifts him off the ground by the village.
2. **Prismatic Breath** – he barrel-rolls out of the dragon's prismatic fire.
3. **First Cut** – a diagonal slash across the dragon's neck.
4. **Tail Sweep** – the dragon spins and its tail knocks him away.
5. **Gathering Shadow** – he climbs high and draws darkness into the blade.
6. **Divine Smite** – a diving overhead strike onto the dragon's back.
7. **Standoff** – the dragon circles off and he lands.

Controls: drag to orbit the camera, scroll or pinch to zoom, space to pause. The buttons along the bottom jump to each beat, and there is a slow-motion toggle.

A rendered video of one full loop is in [`../videos/paladin-vs-opal-dragon.mp4`](../videos/paladin-vs-opal-dragon.mp4).
